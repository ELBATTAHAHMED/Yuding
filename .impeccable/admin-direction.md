# Yuding Admin — direction contract

Mode: Operate. Code-led rebuild of the existing Admin route tree. The user's brief pins the interface hierarchy, neutral light/charcoal dark palettes, brand and sandbox constraints. Confirmed answer: keep the brief's priorities (failed refunds and booking/payment drift first).

THESIS: the travel operator starts with an outstanding incident and investigates the connected dossier without losing their place in the register.

OWN-WORLD: Yuding's five travel verticals, persisted hotel images and itineraries, currency-separated settlement records, cancellation policy and sandbox refund outcome. The console's material is the real itinerary and ledger, not a generated chart or decorative travel backdrop.

STORY: a failed refund leads to its cancellation, original payment, policy and fee; the associated dossier reveals the journey and traveler; accountable decisions stay in the domain's history.

FORM: code-led operational registers and a persistent contextual inspector. Concept seed key d40ed5b7 assigned candidate 6; the brief's pinned palette, hierarchy, logos and operational conventions govern the final direction.

An operator at a desktop during the working day needs to see the outstanding refund immediately, then move into its travel and payment context. A late shift needs the same hierarchy on neutral charcoal surfaces.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a compact full-width Attention requise queue leads the overview; genuine currency-separated metrics form one strip; the real booking-by-vertical distribution and recent travel dossiers sit together; recent payments and an explicit unavailable-telemetry region complete the page. No synthetic historical trend, no full-viewport cards.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: opening a row preserves the list and its URL filters behind one native dialog inspector. The dossier joins travel, traveler, financial ledger and lifecycle through authorized API reads and contextual links. Every entity uses the same inspector geometry, semantic detail/JSON tabs and Escape/focus behavior.

SYSTEM: 272px sidebar / 76px rail; seven destinations in the brief's four groups; official ink logo on light and white logo on dark. 32px desktop content gutters, 24px tablet, 16px mobile. Workhorse Poppins from the incumbent project, 26px page headings, 15px section headings, 12–13px operational text. 7px region and inspector corners, 5px controls, neutral separators. Teal denotes navigation and actions; semantic dots convey status. Numeric columns use tabular figures. 150ms feedback and exit, 220ms panel/sidebar movement; reduced-motion disables transitions.

QUALITY BAR: an obvious composition change from the rejected old Admin; no clipped desktop navigation, fake identities, false availability or merged currencies; no empty full-height region; database pagination with stable sort and server filters; compact mobile records and full-screen inspectors; stored photos or initials; independent downstream and gateway RBAC. Finance retains compatible old URLs through redirects.

Surface boundary: frontend/web/src/app/(admin)/admin, components/admin, styles/admin.css; supporting authenticated services and relevant backend Admin endpoints only. Legacy and unrelated checkout/public work are preserved. No raster assets generated: only visually verified existing official logos, authenticated profile photos and persisted property image URLs.
